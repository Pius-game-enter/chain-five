How to play

  • On your turn, play a card and drop a chip on a matching card on the board. Every card is on the board twice.
  • Make five in a row across, down or diagonally. That is a line. Two lines win.
  • Your second line may share one chip with your first.
  • Corners are free spots that count for everyone.
  • Wild jacks (J♦ J♣) go on any open spot.
  • Remove jacks (J♠ J♥) take away one of your opponent's chips. Chips in a finished line are safe.
  • If both spots for a card are taken, the card is dead. Swap it for a new one without losing your turn.
  • The board is the same every game. Each card is once in the top half and once in the bottom half, in suit and rank order, so you can learn where things are.
